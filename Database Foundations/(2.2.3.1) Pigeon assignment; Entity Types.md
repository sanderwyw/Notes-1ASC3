Board Members
- Attributes
	- Role
		- Can either be chairman, secretary or treasurer
	- Each board member is also a memberp
Member
- Attributes
	- Name
	- Address
	- Phone
	- Email
	- Date of birth
	- National licence number 
		- Issued by the pigeon racing federation
	- Pigeons
		- Foreign key with pigeon, each owner can have multiple owners
- Special Traits
	- Members pay a yearly fee
	- Members that leave must stay in the database
Pigeon
- Attributes
	- Ring number 
		- Ex. BE-24-6104477
		- Country, year of birth, serial number
	- Name
		- Not unique, pigeons may have the same name
	- Sex
	- Colour description
	- Strain
		- Bloodline named after breeder who developed it
	- Sire
		- Father
		- Most often this pigeon is also part of the database
		- A pigeon's parents may be bought abroad in which case its parents may not be known
	- Dam
		- Mother
		- Most often this pigeon is also part of the database
		- A pigeon's parents may be bought abroad in which case its parents may not be known
	- Owner
		- Foreign key with member, each pigeon can have only one owner
Loft
- Attributes
	- Address
	- Geographic coordinates
- Special traits
	- Every pigeon can have at most one loft
	- Pigeons that have their loft changed cannot be raced with anymore and their new loft is therefore not recorded
Vaccination
- Attributes
	- Date of vaccination
	- Vaccine batch number
	- Vet
		- Vet that administered the vaccination
- Special traits
	- Pigeons can only race if they have had a vaccination
Vets
- Attributes
	- Name
	- Practice
	- Phone number
- Special traits
	- Vets are distinctly different from club members
Race
- Attributes
	- Release point
		- Location name (Leuven, Hasselt, Barcelona)
		- Same point is used many times across many seasons
	- Release moment
	- Basketing deadline
	- Entry fee per pigeon
	- Total prize money to be distributed
	- Moment of release
	- Contesting pigeons
		- Foreign key with pigeon
	- Contesting members
		- Foreign key with member
- Special traits
	- Members pay per basketed pigeon
	- Larger races are are flown jointly with neighbouring clubs, but this case only records its own members results, not the national pigeon racing rankings
Race x Pigeon (N-M)
- Attributes
	- Time of arrival
	- Speed m/n
		- Implicit because time of arrival and distance from departure (because coordinates of departure and arrival) are recorded
- Special Traits
	- A pigeon that is basketed but never clocked has no arrival recorded
Race x Member (N-M)
- Attributes
	- Member's pigeon's in the race
Season
- Race (Foreign Key with Race)
- Name
- SeasonID
