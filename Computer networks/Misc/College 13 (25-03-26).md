CIA triad
- confidentially, integrity and availability
- integrity: information is not changed
- availability: information is accessible
- confidentiality: information is private
AAA security
- Authorization: check who the entity is and what they are allowed to do
- Accountability: check who is doing certain things 
- Authentication: check who the person is (password, keys)
DOS attack
- Denial of service
- Overwhelms online server and renders it unusable
Man in the middle:
- All information between person A and person B can be viewed and changed by a man in the middle
DNS attack:
- Changing the website to another website
- For example redirecting all kbc.be users to a website that steals their data
