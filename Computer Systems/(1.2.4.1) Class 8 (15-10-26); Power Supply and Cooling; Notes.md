- Power supply/ PSU
	- AC/DC
		- AC
			- *Alternating* signal
			- From *wall* socket
		- DC
			- *Static* signal
			- Within computer and processor
	- Function power supply
		- *Convert* AC -> DC
		- *Regulate* electricity to fixed levels
		- Send regulated electricity to *specific* components
			- 12V (processor, GPU, fans)
			- 5V (USB, SATA, motherboard)
			- 3.3 (M.2 SSDs, motherboard)
		- Electricity is sent through *connectors* with pins
			- 24-pin: motherboard
			- 4- or 8-pin: processor
			- PCIe: graphics card
			- SATA power: disks
			- These connector types make it so that cables *cannot* be plugged into the *wrong* components
	- Types of power supplies
		- Power
			- Maximum watts it can deliver
			- Sum energy cost of all parts should be equal then or lesser than this
		- Efficiency
			- How much of the input is usable
			- 80 PLUS Bronze -> Titanium
				- Bronze
					- 82% efficiency and higher (derived form 80 PLUS)
					- Parts need 400W
					- **400/ 0.82** = 488W
					- 88W is converted into heat and is functionally lost
