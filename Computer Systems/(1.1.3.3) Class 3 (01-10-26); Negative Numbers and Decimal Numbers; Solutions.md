5.Decimal to binary fractional notation
- 13.25 -> binary notation
	- 1) Sign 
		- = 0 (positive) (sign)
	- 2) To binary
		- Whole
			- 13/2 = 6 (remainder 1)
			- 6/2 = 3 (remainder 0)
			- 3/2 = 1 (remainder 1)
			- 1/2 = 0 (remainder 1)
			- = 1101
		- Fraction
			- 0.25 x 2 = 0.5 (remainder 0)
			- 0.5 x 2 = 1 (remainder 1)
			- = 01
		- Together
			- = 1101.01
	- 3) Normalise 
		- 1101.01 = *1.10101* x 2^3
		- The point (.) shifted 3 places
	- 4) Bias
		- E = **3** + 7 = 10
		- 10 -> binary = 1010
	- 5) Mantissa
		- After the leading 1 = *110101* 
		- Only 3 bits fit -> M = 1(*101*)01
			- Trailing *01* is lost
	- 6) Adding up
		- (0)"1010"{101}
			- () = S (sign)
			- " " = E (exponent)
				- = 10
				- real exponent = 10 - 7 = 3
			- {} = M (mantissa/ value)
				- with implicit leading 1
- 01010101 (fractional notation) -> decimal
	- S = 0 (positive)
	- E = 1010 = 10 - 7 = 3
	- M = 101 with implicit leading 1 = 1.101
	- 1.101 x 2^3 = 1101.0 = 13
		- The original 0.25 has been lost
			- Two possible causes
				- Never-ending fractions
					- Binary pattern repeats *forever* as with 0.1
					- *No number* of bits is ever enough
				- It does not fit
					- The pattern is *longer* than the mantissa (as with 13.25)
					- Only applicable when the pattern in *finite*
