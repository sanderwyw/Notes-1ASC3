	- A1. A component has a resistance of 8ohm and is under a voltage of 24V. Calculate the current and the power.
		- 24V = I * 8
		- 24/8 = I
		- 3 = I
	- A2. A processor consumes 65 W at a voltage of 1.3 V. Calculate both the current and the resistance.
		- Current (I) = 65W / 1.3V = 50A
	- A3. An LED tolerates at most 15 Am and is connected in series with a resistor to a voltage source of 6 V. Which resistor do you need at minimum to limit the current to 15 mA? (ignore the LEDs own voltage)
		- R = V / I = 6 / 0.015 = 400ohm
